#Choose
Choose is an 100% free app for any computer.
Once installed, you can pick sounds for your keyboard and mouse.
After you’ve chosen them, every time you press a key or click your mouse (left or right), your selected sound will play.

Even if you close the app, the sounds will still play — the app simply hides itself when you press “close”.
To open it again (and stop it), click the little arrow in the bottom‑right corner of your screen, then double‑left‑click Choose.

When it opens, go to Settings (top‑left corner) and decide whether you want to stop the keyboard sounds, the mouse sounds, or both.

I’ve added all my favourite meme sounds and more.
You can see a first preview on Instagram and YouTube (@checkmtheory).
But you can also add as many sounds as you like, and delete any you don’t want.

It works with MP3 and WAV files.

If you want to play a prank with Choose, test it, or review it, feel free to record it and post it using #choose.
